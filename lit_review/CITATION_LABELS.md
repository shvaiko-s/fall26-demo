## Assignment Rules

- Keep existing labels unchanged when adding or renaming files.
- Form labels from the initials of authors' last names, in the paper's author order: Haddock, Ma, and Rebrova becomes [HMR].
- When author initials collide, append initials from distinctive title words. For example, Strohmer and Vershynin's “A randomized Kaczmarz method with exponential convergence” can use [SVRK].
- Treat multiple formats or versions of the same paper as one paper with one label.
- Keep removed papers in this record, marking them as removed; do not reuse their labels for different papers.
- These are local reference labels. For example: “Compare the filtering rule in [HMR].”
